# Northstar Commerce Exception Handling Policy
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective Date:** 2025-02-20  
**Source:** internal-policy  
**Document ID:** EXCEPTION-POLICY-1.2

---

## 1. Purpose
Defines the process for handling policy exceptions — cases where standard policy cannot or should not be applied.

---

## 2. Definition of Exception
An exception is any decision that deviates from documented policy rules, including:
- Refund outside eligibility criteria
- Cancellation after standard window
- Fee waiver not covered by policy
- Goodwill gesture beyond standard compensation
- Expedited processing outside SLA

---

## 3. Exception Categories

### 3.1 Customer Loyalty Exception
- Long-term customer (3+ years, 10+ orders)
- High lifetime value (> $5,000)
- First-time issue for this customer
- **Approval:** Tier 2 (Manager), up to $200 goodwill

### 3.2 Carrier Error Acknowledgment
- Carrier admitted fault in writing
- Northstar logistics confirms carrier error
- Customer not at fault
- **Approval:** Tier 2 (Manager), full refund + shipping

### 3.3 First-Time Customer Goodwill
- Customer's first order with Northstar
- Issue not customer-caused
- Goodwill gesture to establish relationship
- **Approval:** Tier 2 (Manager), up to $50 or 20% of order

### 3.4 Exceptional Circumstances
- Medical emergency, natural disaster, death in family
- Verified by customer (documentation requested but not required)
- **Approval:** Tier 3 (Director), case-by-case

### 3.5 System/Process Failure
- Northstar system error caused customer harm
- Billing error, duplicate charge, wrong item shipped
- **Approval:** Tier 2 (Manager), full remediation + goodwill

---

## 4. Exception Request Process

### 4.1 Agent Initiation
1. Agent identifies need for exception
2. Completes Exception Request Form:
   - Customer ID, order ID
   - Exception category (from Section 3)
   - Policy section being excepted
   - Requested outcome
   - Customer impact statement
   - Supporting evidence

### 4.2 Review & Decision
| Reviewer | Authority | Criteria |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Manager | Tier 2 exceptions | Customer history, loyalty, impact |
| Director | Tier 3 exceptions | Precedent, financial impact, policy integrity |
| Legal | Legal/regulatory exceptions | Compliance, liability |

### 4.3 Required Justification
Every exception must document:
- **Why** policy cannot be applied
- **What** alternative resolution is proposed
- **Impact** on customer (financial, experience)
- **Precedent** risk (will this create expectation?)
- **Cost** to Northstar (refund amount, operational)

---

## 5. Documentation & Audit Trail
All exceptions logged with:
- Exception ID (format: EXC-YYYYMMDD-XXXX)
- Requestor, approver, timestamps
- Category, policy section excepted
- Full justification narrative
- Financial impact
- Customer communication record
- Approval chain

---

## 6. Exception Limits & Guardrails

| Guardrail | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max goodwill per customer/year | $500 |
| Max exceptions per agent/month | 10 |
| Exception rate target | < 2% of total resolutions |
| Auto-flag for review | > 3 exceptions same customer/90 days |
| Director notification required | Any exception > $1,000 |

---

## 7. Prohibited Exceptions
- ❌ Override legal/regulatory requirements
- ❌ Bypass anti-fraud controls
- ❌ Violate payment processor terms
- ❌ Create precedent for systemic policy gap (fix policy instead)
- ❌ Approve without documented justification

---

## 8. Policy Gap Resolution
When exceptions reveal systemic policy gaps:
1. Document pattern (3+ similar exceptions in 90 days)
2. Submit Policy Change Request to Policy Team
3. Policy Team reviews within 30 days
4. If approved: policy updated, exception category retired
5. If denied: exception continues with enhanced documentation
