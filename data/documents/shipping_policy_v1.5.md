# Northstar Commerce Shipping Policy
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-10  
**Source:** internal-policy  
**Document ID:** SHIPPING-POLICY-1.5

---

## 1. Purpose
Defines shipping methods, delivery timeframes, and carrier responsibilities for Northstar Commerce orders.

---

## 2. Shipping Methods & Timeframes

| Method | Domestic (US) | International | Cost |
|--------|---------------|---------------|------|
| Standard | 5-7 business days | 10-15 business days | Free on orders $50+ |
| Express | 2-3 business days | 5-7 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | N/A | $24.99 |

**Note:** Timeframes are estimates, not guarantees. Carrier delays beyond our control do not constitute policy violations.

---

## 3. Order Processing
- Orders placed before 2 PM EST: Processed same business day
- Orders placed after 2 PM EST: Processed next business day
- Weekend/holiday orders: Processed next business day
- Processing status: **pending** → **processing** → **shipped**

---

## 4. Carrier Partners
Primary carriers:
- **FedEx:** Ground, Express, Overnight
- **UPS:** Ground, 2nd Day Air, Next Day Air
- **USPS:** Priority Mail, First Class (small items)

Carrier selection based on: destination, package dimensions, shipping method, cost optimization.

---

## 5. Tracking & Visibility
- Tracking number provided via email/SMS within 1 hour of shipment
- Real-time tracking available on order details page
- Delivery notifications sent at: out for delivery, delivered, exception

---

## 6. Delivery Exceptions

### 6.1 Delayed Shipments
- Carrier delay > 3 business days: Proactive customer notification
- Carrier delay > 7 business days: Refund eligibility per Refund Policy Section 4
- Customer may request escalation at any point

### 6.2 Failed Deliveries
- Carrier attempts delivery 3 times over 5 business days
- After 3rd attempt: Package held at local facility for 7 days
- Customer may schedule re-delivery or pickup
- After 7 days: Returned to Northstar Commerce (treated as carrier return)

### 6.3 Lost/Damaged in Transit
- Carrier claim filed automatically by logistics team
- Customer notified within 24 hours of claim filing
- Refund/replacement per Refund Policy Section 5

---

## 7. International Shipping
- Duties/taxes: Customer responsibility (estimated at checkout)
- Customs delays: Not counted in delivery estimates
- Restricted items: Vary by country (checked at checkout)
- Returns: Customer pays return shipping unless defective

---

## 8. Shipping Address Changes
- Before shipment: Free address change via self-service
- After shipment: Carrier intercept fee ($15-25), not guaranteed
- Address verification: Required for orders > $500
