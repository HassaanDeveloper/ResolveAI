# Northstar Commerce Refund Policy
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15  
**Source:** internal-policy  
**Document ID:** REFUND-POLICY-2.1

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the terms and conditions under which Northstar Commerce issues refunds to customers for purchases made through our platforms.

---

## 2. Scope
Applies to all orders placed on northstarcommerce.com, mobile app, and marketplace channels.

---

## 3. General Refund Eligibility

### 3.1 Standard Eligibility Criteria
A refund is eligible when ALL of the following conditions are met:
- Order was placed within the last 30 days from delivery date (or estimated delivery date for delayed shipments)
- Item is in original condition with all accessories and packaging
- Valid proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is provided
- No prior refund has been issued for the same order

### 3.2 Exceptions to Time Limit
- **Defective items:** No time limit for manufacturing defects
- **Delayed shipments:** See Section 4
- **Carrier loss/damage:** See Section 5

---

## 4. Delayed Shipment Refunds

### 4.1 Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- Shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date by more than **7 business days**
- Package has **not been delivered** (tracking shows in_transit, delayed, or failed)
- Customer has not received the item

### 4.2 Auto-Approval Threshold
- Refunds **≤ $100** for delayed shipments: **AUTO_APPROVE**
- Refunds **> $100** for delayed shipments: **REQUIRES_APPROVAL** from manager

### 4.3 Process
1. Customer requests refund citing delayed shipment
2. System verifies tracking shows delay > 7 business days past estimated delivery
3. If eligible amount ≤ $100 → automatic approval and processing
4. If eligible amount > $100 → escalated to manager for approval

---

## 5. Carrier Loss or Damage

### 5.1 Eligibility
Full refund (including shipping costs) when:
- Carrier tracking shows "lost," "damaged," or "returned to sender"
- Carrier claim has been filed by Northstar Commerce logistics team
- Customer confirms non-receipt or damage upon delivery

### 5.2 Approval
- All carrier loss/damage refunds: **REQUIRES_APPROVAL** from manager
- Amounts > $500: **REQUIRES_APPROVAL** from director

---

## 6. Refund Approval Matrix

| Tier | Amount Range | Eligibility Met | Approval Required |
|------|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| 1 (Auto) | ≤ $100 | Yes | None (automatic) |
| 2 (Manager) | $100.01 - $500 | Yes | Manager approval |
| 3 (Director) | $500.01 - $1,000 | Yes | Director approval |
| 4 (Legal) | > $1,000 | Yes | Legal review required |
| Any | Any | No | **DENY** (policy violation) |

---

## 7. Refund Methods and Timing

### 7.1 Original Payment Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within:
- Credit/Debit: 5-10 business days
- Store Credit: 24 hours
- Gift Card: 24 hours

### 7.2 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued for:
- Missing accessories (pro-rated)
- Minor cosmetic damage (up to 20% of item value)
- Price adjustments within 7 days of purchase

---

## 8. Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refund:
- Digital goods (software licenses, gift cards, subscriptions)
- Personalized/customized items (unless defective)
- Final sale / clearance items (marked as such at purchase)
- Items damaged by customer misuse

---

## 9. Policy Violations
Any refund request that violates eligibility criteria will be **DENIED** with a written explanation citing the specific policy section violated.

---

## 10. Audit and Compliance
All refund decisions are logged in the audit trail with:
- Request ID and timestamp
- Policy rule applied
- Approval chain (if applicable)
- Final decision and reason
